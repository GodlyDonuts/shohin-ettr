# DIVERGE-STI1: Stage-Typed Semantic Interface

Status: frozen after RRG1 closure and before STI1 implementation, composite
evaluation, or confirmation access.

## 1. Capability hypothesis

RRG1 reaches 768/768 on natural QUERY across every mode and renderer, but
systematically inverts one EVIDENCE surface. Protected NVE1 already owns the
complete EVIDENCE transaction. STI1 tests the narrower architectural claim:

> Semantic roles require stage-typed owners. A typed composition of the
> qualified NVE1 EVIDENCE owner and qualified RRG1 QUERY owner will provide a
> complete natural interface without universal parameter sharing.

This is structurally different from RRG1. There is no shared REFERENT owner,
no additional training, and no RRG1 data/model variant.

## 2. Frozen composite

One immutable composite runtime loads the existing RRG1 checkpoint but routes
owners as follows:

- WORLD: protected TOL3 source owner;
- EVIDENCE: the complete protected NVE1 numeric and symbol owner;
- EXECUTE: the exact protected rational executor;
- QUERY: the frozen RRG1 length-free relational owner.

Transactions bind type, packet/source identity, provenance, and exact owner
state. Cross-stage parameter storage is forbidden. No owner is trainable.

## 3. Development admission

The opened SRP1 board is development-only. Admission requires all three:

- QUERY at least 765/768;
- EVIDENCE at least 3,070/3,072;
- at least 255/256 fully sealed episodes.

Only a conjunctive pass may open the still-unopened CCR1 board with SHA-256
`299237068f436ba33a68487b5300fcd724f8c98bd8bfe6b1916a4ebc7541ebf7`.

## 4. Fresh confirmation gate

Confirmation retains the RRG1 end-to-end and integrity floors:

- WORLD 256/256, EVIDENCE at least 3,070/3,072, QUERY at least 765/768,
  sealing at least 255/256;
- every query mode at least 254/256 and renderer at least 127/128;
- sensitive, invariant, extensional-parity, no-evidence abstention, and partial
  underdetermination floors at least 254/256;
- at least four more exact queries than frozen SRP1 and no evidence regression;
- QUERY role-slot swap loses at least 90 queries, marker deletion loses at
  least 49, and arbitrary entity renaming changes zero logits/assignments;
- shuffled evidence, state reset, and operation shift each lose at least 50
  points;
- packet/query swaps reject, post-seal poison is invariant, no invalid receipt
  is accepted, and all protected owner hashes remain bit-identical.

## 5. Pass/kill boundary

A miss closes STI1 without owner, threshold, routing, checkpoint, surface,
seed, or evaluator variants. A pass qualifies the natural semantic interface
needed for exactly one branch-local verified-plasticity integration. It does
not authorize continuation pretraining or establish open-domain reasoning.
