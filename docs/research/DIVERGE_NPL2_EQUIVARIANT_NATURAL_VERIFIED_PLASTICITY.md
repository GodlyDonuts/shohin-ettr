# DIVERGE-NPL2: Equivariant Natural Verified Plasticity

Status: PASS on the single development run and all five frozen confirmation
seeds. The exact result and claim boundary are recorded below.

## Capability hypothesis

Oracle PL1 established that 64 session scalars updated by branch-local,
verifier-certified eligibility can recover unseen noncommuting mini-languages
and retain that capability after all demonstrations are deleted. EIC1 now
establishes a source-sensitive, renderer-invariant QUERY transaction owner;
NVE1 already establishes the natural verifier-message EVIDENCE owner.

NPL2 tests the missing conjunction:

> Natural WORLD programs, verifier feedback, and late queries can be compiled
> by the qualified owners into protected typed transactions that drive the
> unchanged PL1 session update and improve held-out deeper composition after
> every source string and feedback message is deleted.

Only the 8 x 8 PL1 policy matrix is mutable. EIC1, NVE1, the structural WORLD
parser, exact executor, verifier, and query reader remain hash-checked and
immutable. NPL2 adds no continuation pretraining or durable consolidation.

## Execution boundary

The structural lexer identifies already-declared aliases, integer spans, and
exact occurrences but has no operation mapping. Every legal verifier message
for a public branch/attempt plan is passed through NVE1; exact scanners expose
candidate spans, while NVE1 alone assigns STEP/VALUE and TARGET/DISTRACTOR
roles. EIC1 compiles each late query into one of the two physical register
identities through its confirmed involution projection.

The supervisor may compare candidate traces to the hidden episode only to
produce a bound PASS/first-error receipt. Candidate policy code receives the
natural message, its authenticated transaction commitment, and no hidden
mapping, trace, terminal state, or answer. After acquisition, all source,
feedback, branch transcript, and residual bytes are deleted; transfer retains
only typed programs, the 64-scalar policy, and late typed QUERY transactions.

## Matched arms and controls

NPL2 reuses the exact PL1 proposal seeds, eight branches, twelve attempts,
verifier-call budget, write projection, score clip, and controls:

- STATIC, CONTEXT_ONLY, DIVERGE_ONLY, FAST_WEIGHT, TRANSIENT_GRAD;
- oracle PL1 as a ceiling;
- NPL2 with natural semantic transactions;
- shuffled feedback, wrong-branch credit, reset, unrelated state transplant,
  no eligibility, poison plus rollback, and protected-owner mutation.

The semantic compiler is run for every legal message before outcome selection,
so hidden verifier outcomes cannot choose which messages receive model
inference. Inference counts, verifier calls, writes, retained bytes, and wall
time are reported.

## Split and gate

Development uses the existing 256 source-disjoint NPL1 episodes. Conditional
confirmation uses five new 256-episode seeds `2026080911` through
`2026080915`, generated and audited before any NPL2 score. Exact alias,
episode, and natural-program identity overlap against all PL1 splits, NPL1
development, and between confirmation seeds must be zero.

The confirmation gate is conjunctive:

1. WORLD, EVIDENCE, and QUERY semantic receipts are each at least 99.5%
   aggregate and 99% per seed/renderer;
2. NPL2 transfer is at least 80% aggregate and 75% per seed;
3. NPL2 remains within five points of oracle PL1 aggregate and on 4/5 seeds;
4. NPL2 exceeds every non-oracle arm by at least ten points aggregate and by
   five points on 4/5 seeds, with paired bootstrap lower bounds above zero;
5. attempt 12 improves at least 50 points over attempt 1;
6. reset loses at least 25 points and returns within three points of STATIC;
7. shuffled, wrong-branch, and unrelated-transplant controls remain within
   three points of STATIC;
8. eligibility removal loses at least five points;
9. poison changes behavior and rollback restores every output/hash;
10. every protected owner hash, verifier receipt, and source-deletion check is
    exact.

Before confirmation, the single 256-episode development run must satisfy the
same aggregate semantic floors, at least 80% late-query exactness, remain
within five points of oracle PL1, beat every non-oracle arm by ten points,
improve at least 50 points from attempt 1 to attempt 12, and reproduce the
reset, shuffled-credit, wrong-branch, no-eligibility, rollback, and protected-
owner controls above. Every legal verifier message is compiled before any
branch outcome is selected; no hidden outcome may trigger model inference.

A development miss closes NPL2 without owner, rank, width, branch, attempt,
seed, renderer, prompt, write-budget, or duration variants. A pass opens the
five frozen confirmation seeds exactly once. Even a confirmation pass is a
controlled synthetic reasoning result, not yet open-domain reasoning or
authorization for long pretraining.
