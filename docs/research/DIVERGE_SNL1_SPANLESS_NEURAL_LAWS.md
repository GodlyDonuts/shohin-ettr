# DIVERGE-SNL1: Spanless Neural Episode Laws

Status: confirmed on development and all five fixed source-disjoint boards.

## Capability Hypothesis

SVE1 confirms that a byte model can emit complete value-bearing evidence and
initialization events without numeric spans or host integer parsing. Its final
law packet still comes from exact intersection over a bounded coefficient
catalog. Separately, the closed NLS1 treatment learned all held episode laws on
its normal development path, but its full gate failed an incorrectly specified
temporal-counterfactual execution condition. That failure remains immutable.

SNL1 asks one new composition question without retraining either owner:

> Can the frozen NLS1 neural synthesizer consume only confirmed SVE1 event
> sequences, commit one coherent episode-local law packet, and preserve exact
> source-deleted recurrent execution on fresh held-out episodes without the
> exact support-intersection solver?

This is a new composition gate, not a reinterpretation or retry of NLS1. The
frozen NLS1 checkpoint, shuffled-outcome control, SVE1 value owner, OQB1 query
owner, and NCP1 command owner remain bit-identical.

## Candidate Path

`WORLD evidence -> OQB1 anonymous register quotient -> SVE1 complete value
events -> frozen NLS1 neural law synthesizer -> one hard law packet -> NCP1
program -> recurrent modular execution -> OQB1 late query`.

The candidate runtime receives no numeric spans, parsed integers, typed
before/after values, typed initial state, typed program, terminal state, or
answer. It contains no exact support intersection. The bounded 25-row output
vocabulary, exact whole-word operation alias binding, exact repeated-register
quotient, and modular matrix application remain engineered and are stated as
limitations.

## Frozen Data And Custody

- development seed: `2026080852`, 256 episodes;
- conditional confirmation seeds: `2026080853`--`2026080857`, 256 episodes
  each;
- all sources, opaque names, and episode identities must be disjoint from the
  complete SVE1 and NLS1 data lineages and between every SNL1 board;
- every board must regenerate byte-for-byte before evaluation;
- the five confirmation assessors remain sealed unless development passes;
- there is no SNL1 training and no parameter/update/threshold search.

A pre-freeze read-only compatibility diagnostic found that the frozen NLS1
treatment recovers `2048/2048` coefficient matrices on the already-open SVE1
development assessor when given complete events. This admitted the new gate but
is not counted as SNL1 evidence. Zero-shot reuse of NCP1 as an evidence alias
pointer scored only `37/6144 = 0.6022%`, so exact operation binding remains an
explicit boundary rather than being silently claimed as learned.

## Frozen Arms And Gate

Positive arms are treatment, unseen register renaming, and coherent register
table reindex. Each must reach at least 99% exact evidence events, initial
events, query binding, complete law packets, terminal states, and answers, with
at least 95% terminal-state exactness at every depth 12--32. NCP1 complete
program exactness must be at least 99%.

The following causal controls are fixed:

- cross-owner table reindex: at most 5% states and 10% answers;
- digit-byte value scrub: at most 1% states and answers;
- occurrence-level quotient break: at most 20% states and 35% answers;
- independently trained shuffled SVE1 event model: at most 5% states;
- independently trained shuffled-outcome NLS1 model: at most 5% states;
- after-value scrub before law synthesis: at most 5% states;
- one demonstration per operation: at most 20% states;
- law-packet reset: exactly 0% states.

All parent hashes and model states must remain bit-identical. Candidate source
must remain spanless and contain neither exact support intersection nor a raw
integer parser. A development miss closes SNL1 without model, seed, threshold,
renderer, control, or evaluator variants. A pass opens all five fixed
confirmation boards once; all five must pass unchanged.

## Claim Boundary

A confirmed pass would qualify only the controlled composition of model-owned
spanless value extraction and neural unseen-law induction. It would remove the
exact law-support solver from this path. It would not establish learned alias
resolution, unrestricted arithmetic, open-domain natural-language reasoning,
or public benchmark capability.
